### Alerting Philosophy

<Callout type="info" title="Effective Alerts">
Alerts based on **symptoms** (user impact), not causes (high CPU).
</Callout>

- Multi-window, multi-burn-rate alerting
- Runbooks linked to each alert
- Severity based on user impact
- Smart routing by team and schedule
